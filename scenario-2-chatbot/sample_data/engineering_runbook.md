## Normal operating range

Pump PX-41 normally operates between 42 and 55 psi. Stop the pump when discharge pressure exceeds 70 psi for more than 10 seconds.

## Troubleshooting

If vibration exceeds 7 mm/s RMS, inspect coupling alignment and bearing temperature before restarting. Record the inspection in the maintenance log.
